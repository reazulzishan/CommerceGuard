# CommerceGuard Ep 2 · three storylines

## A · "One parcel, two phones"  ← BUILT (recommended: a stranger follows two people and one parcel)
Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: split screen to one.
Nusrat in Dhaka orders a dress on cash on delivery from Rafi's small online shop. Their chat plays out on two phones while the real footage of each person plays beside it: the order, three days of waiting, "this looks fake", the parcel refused at the door and sent back. Then the camera pulls out to why: the 2021 collapse that made Bangladesh pay cash at the door. CommerceGuard's three models each read one part of this same story, and the film ends by replaying the chat with live risk scores on every message.
First 2 s: Dhaka from above, a chat bubble pops in: "ভাই, অর্ডার করছি, ক্যাশ অন ডেলিভারি তো?"
Beats: hook (chat in Dhaka) → the parcel comes back → why: 2021 trust collapse → COD as the shield, sellers carry the risk → model 1 reads the customer → model 2 scores the order → engine ranks the day's orders → the chat replayed with scores → honest limits → next.
Look: graphite ground, real footage in rounded frames, a neutral chat UI in our palette, Bangla type in Noto Sans Bengali, amber for the one number.
Sound: calm ambient bed, message pops on each bubble, a single low hit when the parcel is refused.

## B · "Tk 3,000 crore" (data journalism)
Device: a number lands. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
Opens on the figure officials quoted for money stuck in Evaly, e-Orange and Dhamaka, then rebuilds the frame into the COD market, the return rates sellers report, and the models' results as numbers that land one by one.
Not built: strongest numbers, but no people to follow.

## C · "The parcel's journey" (bold)
Device: the object is the explanation. Form: one continuous take.
The camera never cuts; it follows one parcel from the packing table to the rider to the door and back, and each stop is where one model looks at the risk.
Not built: beautiful, but needs footage of one continuous delivery we don't have.
